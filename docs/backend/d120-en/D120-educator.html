D120 educator map · Traceable Mathematical Work

English planning surface for 14 assessments, 14 rubrics, 79 criteria, six credential-state definitions, and five evaluator roles using the same semantic IDs as the Indonesian D120 course.

Open educator data · Open learner data

Truth boundary: credential states are definitions, not achievement claims. External action is never inferred from a template, locator, or prepared artifact.

Assessment designs

Bounded community transaction or externally reviewed local equivalent authentic_delivery

A single small, non-duplicative, reviewable contribution, submitted only with separate human authorization; if external action is unsafe or unavailable, use independent local review and a weaker label.

Instructions: Record the actual state: prepared, submitted, acknowledged, accepted, merged, published, rejected, withdrawn, unknown, or externally_reviewed_local. Acceptance is not required and must not be inferred.

O017-ASMT-BOUNDED-COMMUNITY-TRANSACTION · 33 outcomes · 5 evidence specifications

  1. O017-CRIT-BOUNDED-COMMUNITY-TRANSACTION-01 — One target, one contribution, the version, locators, duplicate search, rights, and what will remain untouched are narrowly specified.
  2. O017-CRIT-BOUNDED-COMMUNITY-TRANSACTION-02 — The diagnosis, minimal change, validation, attribution, limitations, withdrawal route, and one concise message can be reviewed as a single package.
  3. O017-CRIT-BOUNDED-COMMUNITY-TRANSACTION-03 — Each external action has separate human authorization for a specific target; without authorization, the package stops locally.
  4. O017-CRIT-BOUNDED-COMMUNITY-TRANSACTION-04 — Each external transition has event evidence; prepared is not called submitted, and submitted is not called accepted, merged, or published.
  5. O017-CRIT-BOUNDED-COMMUNITY-TRANSACTION-05 — If external action is unsafe or unavailable, an independent checker assesses the actual package and the result is labeled externally_reviewed_local, with no claim of community participation.
Independent author–referee cycle authentic_delivery

One review cycle with two different people serving as author and referee.

Instructions: Preserve the initial artifact, report, response, disposition table, revision, before/after evidence, identities or private IDs, and unresolved issues.

O017-ASMT-INDEPENDENT-REFEREE-CYCLE · 32 outcomes · 5 evidence specifications

  1. O017-CRIT-INDEPENDENT-REFEREE-CYCLE-01 — The learner or author and the referee are two different people; their real identities or private IDs can be audited by the course assessor.
  2. O017-CRIT-INDEPENDENT-REFEREE-CYCLE-02 — The initial artifact, referee report, author's response, and final revision are frozen as separate versions without overwriting history.
  3. O017-CRIT-INDEPENDENT-REFEREE-CYCLE-03 — Each significant comment identifies the target, locator, diagnosis, evidence, severity, confidence, and an executable action.
  4. O017-CRIT-INDEPENDENT-REFEREE-CYCLE-04 — Each comment is mapped to a decision, action, evidence, change location, status, and a reason if rejected or held.
  5. O017-CRIT-INDEPENDENT-REFEREE-CYCLE-05 — Significant changes are checked against the initial and final artifacts; their impact and unresolved issues remain visible.
Live seminar authentic_delivery

Attendance at or delivery of an actual mathematics seminar, or an accessible supervised equivalent, with an honest evidence record.

Instructions: Keep event evidence, timestamped notes, a dependency map, a question log, unavailable portions, corrections, and accessibility surfaces.

O017-ASMT-LIVE-SEMINAR · 22 outcomes · 5 evidence specifications

  1. O017-CRIT-LIVE-SEMINAR-01 — The event identity, time, learner's role, evidence of attendance or delivery, and limitations of the record can be checked.
  2. O017-CRIT-LIVE-SEMINAR-02 — The notes distinguish observations, verification, questions, objections, and inaudible or unavailable portions without inventing content.
  3. O017-CRIT-LIVE-SEMINAR-03 — The main claims, definitions, assumptions, lemmas, proofs, and open problems are mapped with sufficiently precise locators or timestamps.
  4. O017-CRIT-LIVE-SEMINAR-04 — The question log and corrected final notes preserve changes, their grounds, and unresolved items.
  5. O017-CRIT-LIVE-SEMINAR-05 — If live audio or video is unavailable, a supervised equivalent preserves the learning objectives and is labeled as an equivalent, not as an event that did not occur.
Real-source erratum dossier authentic_delivery

Audit one real, openly accessible source with its version frozen, with a reproducible diagnosis, minimal correction, and independent mathematical check.

Instructions: Submission is not required and must not be inferred. Keep the version, rights, reproducer, correction, validation, independent check, impact, and uncertainty.

O017-ASMT-REAL-ERRATUM-DOSSIER · 41 outcomes · 5 evidence specifications

  1. O017-CRIT-REAL-ERRATUM-DOSSIER-01 — The work, version, copy, locator, wording, check date, and rights of the actual source can be checked.
  2. O017-CRIT-REAL-ERRATUM-DOSSIER-02 — The reproducer or reconstruction checks all hypotheses, demonstrates the failure minimally, and separates observation from proof.
  3. O017-CRIT-REAL-ERRATUM-DOSSIER-03 — The proposed correction is the smallest sufficient change and is justified by a mathematical argument, without overstating its scope.
  4. O017-CRIT-REAL-ERRATUM-DOSSIER-04 — A different person checks the diagnosis and correction, recording the decision, its grounds, their identity or private ID, and any remaining disagreement.
  5. O017-CRIT-REAL-ERRATUM-DOSSIER-05 — The dossier distinguishes prepared, checked, and submitted; submission is not claimed without evidence, and impact beyond the scope remains unknown.
Real-source search authentic_delivery

A search without a pre-seeded candidate list for one actual mathematics paper or monograph, whose version is frozen.

Instructions: Keep the question, queries, retrieval times, stopping rule, version, rights, authority comparison, decisions, and uncertainty.

O017-ASMT-REAL-SOURCE-SEARCH · 21 outcomes · 5 evidence specifications

  1. O017-CRIT-REAL-SOURCE-SEARCH-01 — One actual mathematics paper or monograph is frozen with a checkable work identity, version, copy, locator, retrieval date, and basis for rights.
  2. O017-CRIT-REAL-SOURCE-SEARCH-02 — The question, all queries, discovery path, relevant negative results, and stopping rule are recorded without accepting a supplied candidate list as a substitute for searching.
  3. O017-CRIT-REAL-SOURCE-SEARCH-03 — At least two candidate surfaces or sources are compared against a specific question; reputation and result ranking are not used as proof.
  4. O017-CRIT-REAL-SOURCE-SEARCH-04 — The statement that a source contains a claim is separated from the decision that the claim is true, with explicit locators, grounds, and limits of inference.
  5. O017-CRIT-REAL-SOURCE-SEARCH-05 — Missing fields, version conflicts, inclusion decisions, and reasons for stopping are preserved without guessing.
