# Worksheet 5 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05}

## Practice exercises {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-practice}

### Exercise 5.1 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-01}

<!-- upstream_entity: Homogenes Polynom/Skalarmultiplikation auf Nullstellenmenge/Abgeschlossen/Aufgabe -->

Let $F\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ be a homogeneous polynomial with zero locus
$V(F)$. Prove that for every point $P\in V(F)$ and every scalar
$\lambda\in K$, we also have $\lambda P\in V(F)$.

### Exercise 5.2 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-02}

<!-- upstream_entity: Homogene Polynome in zwei Variablen/C/X^n-Y^n/Aufgabe -->

Determine the factorisation of the polynomial

$$
X^n-Y^n\in\mathbb C[X,Y]
$$

for $n\in\mathbb N_+$.

### Exercise 5.3 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-03}

<!-- upstream_entity: Homogene Polynome in zwei Variablen/Algebraisch abgeschlossener Körper/Zerfällt/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field and $F\in K[X,Y]$ a homogeneous
polynomial. Prove that $F$ splits into linear factors.

### Exercise 5.4 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-04}

<!-- upstream_entity: Homogenes Polynom/Körper/Faktoren/Homogen/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field, $F\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ a homogeneous polynomial, and
$F=GH$ a factorisation. Prove that $G$ and $H$ are also homogeneous.

### Exercise 5.5 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-05}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring/Mehrere Variablen/Potenzen des maximalen Ideals/Gradabschnitt/Aufgabe -->

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and

$$
P=R[X_1,\ldots,X_m]
$$

the polynomial ring in $m$ variables over $R$. Let

$$
\mathfrak m=(X_1,\ldots,X_m)
$$

be the ideal generated by the variables. Prove that

$$
\mathfrak m^n=P_{\ge n},
$$

where $P_{\ge n}$ denotes the ideal of $P$ generated by all homogeneous
polynomials of degree $\ge n$.

### Exercise 5.6 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-06}

<!-- upstream_entity: Homogene Polynome/Unter linearer Transformation/Bleibt homogen/Aufgabe -->

Prove that a homogeneous polynomial remains homogeneous of the same degree
under a linear change of variables, whereas this need not hold under an
affine-linear change of variables.

### Exercise 5.7 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-07}

<!-- upstream_entity: Affine Varietäten/Affin-linear äquivalente Nullstellengebilde/Ist Äquivalenzrelation/Aufgabe -->

Prove that affine-linear equivalence is an equivalence relation on the affine
algebraic sets $V,V'\subseteq\mathbb A_K^n$.

### Exercise 5.8 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-08}

<!-- upstream_entity: Affine Ebene/Punkt und verschiedene Geraden/Transformation auf Achsenkreuz/Aufgabe -->

Let $P=(a,b)$ be a point in the affine plane, and $L,L'$ two distinct lines
through $P$. Let $C=V(F)$, with $F\in K[X,Y]$, be a plane algebraic curve.
Explicitly describe a change of variables (a change of coordinates) such
that, in the new coordinates, $P$ is the origin and the two lines are the
coordinate axes. What is the curve equation in the new coordinates?

### Exercise 5.9 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-09}

<!-- upstream_entity: Affine Ebene/Drei verschiedene Geraden/Affin-linear äquivalent/Aufgabe -->

Let $C$ and $D$ each be a plane affine algebraic curve consisting of the
union of three distinct lines, with the three lines of each curve meeting
at one point. Prove that an affine-linear change of coordinates takes $C$ to $D$.

### Exercise 5.10 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-10}

<!-- upstream_entity: Affine Ebene/Vier verschiedene Geraden/Nicht affin-linear äquivalent/Aufgabe -->

Let $C$ and $D$ each be a plane affine algebraic curve consisting of the
union of four distinct lines, with the four lines of each curve meeting
at one point. Prove that in general no affine-linear change of coordinates
takes $C$ to $D$.

### Exercise 5.16 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-16}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomiale Abbildungen der Ebene/x nach t^2+s-3, y nach 3ts+s^2-t/Bild von x^2y+3xy-y^3/Aufgabe -->

Calculate the image $\widetilde F$ of the polynomial

$$
F=X^2Y+3XY-Y^3
$$

under the substitution homomorphism

$$
K[X,Y]\longrightarrow K[S,T]
$$

determined by

$$
X\longmapsto T^2+S-3,
\qquad
Y\longmapsto 3TS+S^2-T.
$$

### Exercise 5.17 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-17}

<!-- upstream_entity: K unendlich/Polynom/Bild unendlich oder konstant/Zwei Beweise/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be an infinite field and $F\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ a polynomial with
associated map

$$
F:\mathbb A_K^n\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^1.
$$

Prove, both with and without Theorem 5.10, that the image of $F$ consists of
one point or infinitely many points.

### Exercise 5.18 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-18}

<!-- upstream_entity: Endlicher Körper/Endlich viele Punkte in Ebene/Parametrisierung und Anzahl/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a finite field with $q$ elements, and let

$$
P_1,\ldots,P_n\in\mathbb A_K^2
$$

be $n$ distinct points in the affine plane. Prove that there is a polynomial map

$$
\varphi:\mathbb A_K^1\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^2
$$

with

$$
\operatorname{bild}\varphi=\{P_1,\ldots,P_n\}
$$

if and only if $1\le n\le q$.

> **Edition note.** The source's count is understood to be a count of
> distinct points; otherwise repetitions would make the criterion in terms
> of $n$ false. Its notation $\operatorname{bild}$ is retained here.

### Exercise 5.19 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-19}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomiale Abbildung/A^2 nach A^1/Eine Faser reduzibel, sonst irreduzibel/Aufgabe -->

Give an example of a polynomial map

$$
\mathbb A_K^2\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^1
$$

such that the inverse image of one point is reducible, while the inverse
image of every other point is irreducible.

### Exercise 5.20 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-20}

<!-- upstream_entity: Körper/Nullstellen auf Polynomkoeffizienten/Eigenschaften/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field. For each $n\in\mathbb N_+$, consider the map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi:K^n&\longrightarrow K^n,\\
(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\ldots,\lambda_n)
&\longmapsto(c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_{n-1}),
\end{aligned}
$$

which sends a tuple of roots $(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$ to the tuple of
coefficients $(c_0,\ldots,c_{n-1})$, omitting the coefficient $1$, of the
monic polynomial

$$
(X-\lambda_1)(X-\lambda_2)\cdots(X-\lambda_n)
=P=c_0+c_1X+\cdots+c_{n-1}X^{n-1}+X^n.
$$

1. Describe $\varphi$ explicitly for $n=2$.
2. Describe $\varphi$ explicitly for $n=3$.
3. Explain why these maps $\varphi$ are polynomial.
4. Prove that the fibres of $\varphi$ are finite.
5. When is the fibre over a tuple $(c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_{n-1})$ empty?
6. What is the maximum number of elements in a fibre? Give examples showing
   that this maximum is attained for $K=\mathbb R$.
7. Now let $K$ be algebraically closed. Prove that $\varphi$ is surjective.

$$
E=V(2x^2+3y^2+4z^2-5)

### Exercise 5.27 - 4 points {#br-ak-2025-2026-w05-ex-27}
