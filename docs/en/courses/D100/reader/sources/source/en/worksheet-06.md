### Exercise 6.1 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-01}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebener polynomialer Graph/Parametrisiere/Aufgabe -->

Let $F(x)\in K[x]$ be a polynomial in one variable over a field $K$. Parametrise the graph of $F$ by polynomials.

### Exercise 6.2 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-02}

<!-- upstream_entity: Affine ebene Kurve/-3t^2+4t-2 und 2t^2+5t-3/Finde Gleichung/Aufgabe -->

$$
x=-3t^2+4t-2,
\qquad
y=2t^2+5t-3.
$$

### Exercise 6.3 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-03}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene Kurve/Rationale Parametrisierung/t^2+t,t^3/Gleichung/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field. Consider the parametrisation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb A_K^1&\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^2,\\
t&\longmapsto(t+t^2,t^3)=(x,y).
\end{aligned}
$$

Determine a nontrivial algebraic equation satisfied by every image point of this map. Also give a point in the affine plane that does not lie on the image curve.

### Exercise 6.4 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-04}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene Kurve/Rationale Parametrisierung/t^2+1,t^3-t/Gleichung/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field. Consider the polynomial map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb A_K^1&\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^2,\\
t&\longmapsto(t^2+1,t^3-t).
\end{aligned}
$$

Determine a nontrivial algebraic equation satisfied by every image point of this map.

The phenomenon described in the following exercise cannot occur over an algebraically closed field.

### Exercise 6.5 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-05}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene Kurve/R/Parametrisiert/Halbabdeckung/Aufgabe -->

Give an example of a polynomial parametrisation

$$
\varphi:\mathbb A_{\mathbb R}^1\longrightarrow
C\subset\mathbb A_{\mathbb R}^2,
$$

where $C$ is the Zariski closure of the image, such that infinitely many points of $C$ are not in the image.

### Exercise 6.6 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-06}

Show that, in the situation of Example 6.3, the given parametrisation of the curve is surjective for $K=\mathbb R$.

### Exercise 6.7 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-07}

<!-- upstream_entity: Homogene Polynome/2 Variablen/Gleicher Grad/Homogene Gleichung/Fakt/Beweis/Aufgabe -->

Let

$$
P_1,P_2,P_3\in K[S,T]
$$

be three homogeneous polynomials of the same degree. Show that there is a homogeneous polynomial

$$
F\in K[X,Y,Z],
\qquad
F\ne0,
$$

such that

$$
F(P_1,P_2,P_3)=0.
$$

The following exercises use the notion of a differentiable curve as discussed in Analysis 2.

### Exercise 6.11 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-11}

### Exercise 6.14 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-14}

<!-- upstream_entity: Differenzierbare Kurve/Bild ist Achsenkreuz/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 6.15 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-15}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene rationale Kurven/Nicht konstantes Bild davon/Ist auch rational/Aufgabe -->

Let $\varphi:\mathbb A_K^2\to\mathbb A_K^2$ be a polynomial map and let $C$ be a plane rational curve. Suppose that $\varphi$ does not map $C$ to a single point. Show that

$$
\overline{\varphi(C)}
$$

is also a rational curve.

### Exercise 6.16 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-16}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene algebraische Kurven/Parametrisierung der Form (t^2,F(t))/Wann injektiv/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, and suppose a polynomial map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi:\mathbb A_K^1&\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^2,\\
t&\longmapsto(t^2,\psi(t)),
\end{aligned}
$$

with $\psi(t)\in K[t]$ is given. Show that $\varphi$ is injective if and only if $\psi$ has the form

$$
\psi(t)=at^n+\theta(t),
$$

where $a\ne0$, $n$ is odd, and $\theta(t)$ is a polynomial containing only even powers.

### Exercise 6.17 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-17}

<!-- upstream_entity: Wahrscheinlichkeit/Zielwahrscheinlichkeit/Kurve/Aufgabe -->

Let $p\in[0,1]$ be the probability that a certain event $A$ occurs in an experiment, so that $1-p$ is the probability that it does not occur ($\neg A$). The experiment is performed twice independently. The combined events

$$
(A,A),\ (A,\neg A),\ (\neg A,A),\ (\neg A,\neg A)
$$

have probabilities depending on $p$. Regard this dependence as the polynomial map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi:\mathbb A_K^1&\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^4,\\
p&\longmapsto
\bigl(p^2,p(1-p),(1-p)p,(1-p)^2\bigr)=(x,y,z,w).
\end{aligned}
$$

1. Show that this map is injective.
2. Describe its image completely by polynomial equations.

### Exercise 6.18 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-18}

<!-- upstream_entity: Wahrscheinlichkeit/Zielwahrscheinlichkeit/Ebene/Aufgabe -->

Let $p\in[0,1]$ be the probability that event $A$ occurs in a first experiment, and let $q\in[0,1]$ be the probability that event $B$ occurs in a second experiment. The two experiments are performed independently. The combined events

$$
(A,B),\ (A,\neg B),\ (\neg A,B),\ (\neg A,\neg B)
$$

have probabilities depending on $p$ and $q$. Regard this dependence as the polynomial map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi:\mathbb A_K^2&\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^4,\\
(p,q)&\longmapsto
\bigl(pq,p(1-q),(1-p)q,(1-p)(1-q)\bigr)=(x,y,z,w).
\end{aligned}
$$

1. Show that this map is injective.
2. Describe its image completely by polynomial equations.

### Exercise 6.19 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-19}

<!-- upstream_entity: Monomiale Abbildung/s,t^2/s,t/xy ist z^2/Aufgabe -->

Consider the map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb A_K^2\supseteq D(s)&\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^3,\\
(s,t)&\longmapsto\left(s,\frac{t^2}{s},t\right)=(x,y,z).
\end{aligned}
$$

Determine an algebraic equation $F$ for its image. Investigate the injectivity and surjectivity of this map as a map to $V(F)$. Compare it with the maps discussed in Exercise 6.29.

### Exercise 6.20 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-20}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring/Homogenisierung und Dehomogenisierung/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ the polynomial ring over $K$ in $n$ variables, and $K[X_1,\ldots,X_n,Z]$ the polynomial ring in $n+1$ variables. For $F\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$, let $\widehat F\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n,Z]$ be its homogenisation with respect to $Z$. For $G\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n,Z]$, let $\widetilde G$ be its dehomogenisation given by $Z\mapsto1$. Show that

$$
\widetilde{\widehat F}=F,
$$

but that it need not be the case that

$$
\widehat{\widetilde G}=G.
$$

### Exercise 6.21 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-21}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring/Dehomogenisierung/Gleichheit und gleicher Grad/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field and $K[X_1,\ldots,X_n,Z]$ the polynomial ring over $K$ in $n+1$ variables. Let

$$
G,H\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n,Z]
$$

be homogeneous polynomials of the same degree. If their dehomogenisations with respect to $Z$ satisfy

$$
\widetilde G=\widetilde H,
$$

show that $G=H$.

### Exercise 6.22 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-22}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring/Homogenisierung/Multiplikativ/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ the polynomial ring over $K$ in $n$ variables, and $K[X_1,\ldots,X_n,Z]$ the polynomial ring in $n+1$ variables. Show that homogenisation with respect to $Z$ is compatible with multiplication.

### Exercise 6.23 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-23}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring/Dehomogenisierung/Einsetzungshomomorphismus/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ the polynomial ring over $K$ in $n$ variables, and $K[X_1,\ldots,X_n,Z]$ the polynomial ring in $n+1$ variables. Describe dehomogenisation with respect to $Z$ as a substitution homomorphism.

### Exercise 6.24 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-24}

Formulate and prove a division-with-remainder statement for homogeneous polynomials in two variables over a field.

### Exercise 6.25 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-25}

<!-- upstream_entity: Homogene Polynome in zwei Variablen/Division mit Rest/Beispiel/1/Aufgabe -->

Let

$$
F=X^4+9X^3Y+7X^2Y^2+XY^3+8Y^4
$$

and

$$
G=X^3+5X^2Y.
$$

Find homogeneous polynomials $Q,R\in\mathbb Q[X,Y]$, with $Q\ne0$, such that

$$
F=GQ+R.
$$

### Exercise 6.29 - 5 points {#br-ak-2025-2026-w06-ex-29}

<!-- upstream_entity: Monomiale Abbildung/S,ST^2,ST/Gleichung für Bild/Aufgabe -->

Consider the two maps

$$
(s,t)\longmapsto(s^2,t^2,st)=(x,y,z)
$$

and

$$
(s,t)\longmapsto(s,st^2,st)=(x,y,z).
$$
