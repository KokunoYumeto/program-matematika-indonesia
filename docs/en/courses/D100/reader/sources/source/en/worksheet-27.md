# Worksheet 27 {#br-ak-2012-w27}

## Warm-up exercises {#br-ak-2012-w27-practice}

### Exercise 27.1 {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-01}

Define an equivalence relation on the set

$$
\mathbb A_K^{n+1}\setminus\{0\}
$$

such that its quotient by this equivalence relation is $n$-dimensional projective space.

<!-- upstream_entity: Projektiver Raum/Projektiv-linearer Unterraum/Gebe Definition/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.2 {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-02}

Define the notion of a *projective linear subspace* of a projective space $\mathbb P_K^n$.

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring/Homogenes Ideal/Von homogenen Elementen erzeugt/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.3 {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-03}

Let

$$
\mathfrak a\subseteq K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]
$$

be an ideal. Show that $\mathfrak a$ is homogeneous if and only if $\mathfrak a$ is generated by homogeneous elements.

<!-- upstream_entity: Projektiver Raum/Endlicher Körper/Anzahl der Elemente/Zweifache Berechnung/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.4 {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-04}

Let

$$
K=\mathbb F_q
$$

be a finite field with $q$ elements. Compute the number of elements of projective space $\mathbb P_K^n$ in two different ways.

The following three exercises concern the Zariski topology on projective spaces.

<!-- upstream_entity: Projektiver Raum/Zariskitopologie/Ist Topologie/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.5 {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-05}

Show that the Zariski topology on projective space is indeed a topology.

<!-- upstream_entity: Projektiver Raum/K/D+a ist leer/Charakterisiere/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.6 {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-06}

Let $K$ be an infinite field and let $\mathbb P_K^n$ be projective space. Characterise the homogeneous ideals $\mathfrak a$ satisfying

$$
D_+(\mathfrak a)=\varnothing.
$$

<!-- upstream_entity: Projektiver Raum/K/Unendlicher Körper/Irreduzibel/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.7 {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-07}

Let $K$ be an infinite field. Show that projective space $\mathbb P_K^n$ is irreducible.

### Exercise 27.8 (3 points) {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-08}

Show that two distinct points $P$ and $Q$ in the projective plane uniquely determine a projective line containing both. How is the equation of this line computed from the coordinates of the two points?

Determine the homogeneous equation of the line through the points

$$
(2,3,7)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
(1,5,-2).
$$

### Exercise 27.9 (3 points) {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-09}

Let $\mathbb P_K^n$ be a projective space of dimension $n$, and let

$$
X,Y\subseteq\mathbb P_K^n
$$

be projective linear subspaces of dimensions $r$ and $s$, respectively. Suppose that

$$
r+s\geq n.
$$

Show that

$$
X\cap Y\ne\varnothing.
$$

<!-- upstream_entity: Projektiver Raum/Unendlicher Körper/Endlich viele Punkte in affiner Umgebung/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.10 (3 points) {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-10}

Let $K$ be an infinite field and let

$$
P_1,\ldots,P_m
$$

be a finite collection of points in projective space $\mathbb P_K^n$. Show that there is a homogeneous linear form

$$
L\in K[X_0,\ldots,X_n]
$$

such that all these points belong to the open set $D_+(L)$.

The next exercise requires an additional definition.

For a homogeneous ideal $I$ in

$$
R=A[X_0,\ldots,X_n]
$$

with the standard grading, the *saturation* of $I$ is defined as

$$
\left\{r\in R\ \middle|\
\text{there exists }k\text{ such that }
r(R_+)^k\subseteq I\right\}.
$$

Here $R_+$ is the irrelevant ideal

$$
\bigoplus_{d\geq1}R_d=(X_0,\ldots,X_n).
$$

<!-- upstream_entity: Saturierung eines homogenen Ideals/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 27.11 (3 points) {#br-ak-2012-w27-ex-11}

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and let

$$
R=A[X_0,\ldots,X_n]
$$

be the polynomial ring with the standard grading. Show that the saturation of a homogeneous ideal $I$ is again a homogeneous ideal.
