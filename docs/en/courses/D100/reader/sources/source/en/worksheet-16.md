### Exercise 16.6 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w16-ex-06}

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field. Consider the affine plane
$\mathbb A_K^2$ together with the $x$-axis

$$
V=V(y).
$$

Show that the following set is a saturated multiplicative system:

$$
S=
\left\{
f\in K[X,Y]
\mathrel{\Big|}
\text{the homogeneous component }f_{\deg(f)}
\text{ contains }x^{\deg(f)}
\right\}.
$$

Sketch the zero loci of some polynomials belonging to $S$ and some not
belonging to $S$.

Let $F$ be the associated topological filter. Compare $F$ with the
neighbourhood filter of $V$ and the generic filter of $V$.

### Exercise 16.8 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w16-ex-08}

Let $U$ be a quasi-affine variety over an algebraically closed field $K$
and let

$$
\psi:U\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^n
$$

be a morphism. Show that $\psi$ factors through the closed subset

$$
V(\mathfrak a)\subseteq\mathbb A_K^n
$$

if and only if $\mathfrak a$ is contained in the kernel of the global
ring homomorphism

$$
\widetilde\psi:
K[T_1,\ldots,T_n]\longrightarrow\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U).
$$

### Exercise 16.13 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w16-ex-13}

Show that the map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi:V(Z^2+W^2-1)&\longrightarrow V(X^2+Y^2-1),\\
(Z,W)&\longmapsto(Z^2-W^2,2ZW)=(X,Y)
\end{aligned}
$$

is a morphism from the unit circle to itself. Show that the preimage of
every point

$$
P\in V(X^2+Y^2-1)
$$

consists of two points.

**Edition note:** the two-point assertion needs additional hypotheses,
for example that $K$ is algebraically closed and
$\operatorname{char}(K)\ne2$. In characteristic $2$ the map is constant;
over $\mathbb Q$, the point $(0,1)$ has no preimage. The source does not
state these restrictions.

### Exercise 16.17 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w16-ex-17}

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, let

$$
\mathfrak a=(f_1,\ldots,f_n)
$$

be a finitely generated ideal, and let $f\in R$. The $R$-algebra

$$
A=
R[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1T_1+\cdots+f_nT_n+f)
$$

is called the *forcing algebra* for $f_1,\ldots,f_n,f$. Show that $A$ has
the following property: for every ring homomorphism $\varphi:R\to S$
to a commutative ring $S$ satisfying

$$
\varphi(f)\in\mathfrak aS,
$$

there is an $R$-algebra homomorphism

$$
\vartheta:A\longrightarrow S.
$$

Show also that this homomorphism is *not* uniquely determined in general.

**Edition note:** the source's non-uniqueness statement is understood in
this general sense. Particular data can force uniqueness, for example
$n=1$ and $f_1=1$.

$$
A=
R[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1T_1+\cdots+f_nT_n+f)
$$

be the associated forcing algebra. Characterise the fibres of the morphism

$$
K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(A)
\longrightarrow
K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(R).
$$

### Exercise 16.19 (4 points) {#br-ak-2025-2026-w16-ex-19}

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, let $R,S$ be $K$-algebras of
finite type that are integral domains, and let

$$
\varphi:R\longrightarrow S
$$

be a $K$-algebra homomorphism, with associated morphism

$$
\varphi^*:
K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(S)
\longrightarrow
K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(R).
$$

Show that the following statements are equivalent.

1. $\varphi$ is injective.
2. The image of $\varphi^*$ is dense in $K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(R)$.
3. $\varphi$ induces a ring homomorphism $Q(R)\to Q(S)$.

### Exercise 16.23 (3 points) {#br-ak-2025-2026-w16-ex-23}

Let

$$
U\subseteq K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(R)
$$

be a quasi-affine variety and let $f\in\Gamma(U,\mathcal O)$ be an
algebraic function. Let

$$
f=g_i/h_i,
\qquad i=1,\ldots,n,
$$

be local representations of $f$ on open sets $D(h_i)\subseteq U$ covering
$U$. Show that its zero fibre is the closed subset

$$
f^{-1}(0)=V(h_1g_1,\ldots,h_ng_n)\cap U.
$$

**Edition note:** on the left of the local representation, the source
writes $q=g_i/h_i$, although the function defined and being represented is
$f$. This edition displays the variable determined by the context of the
exercise.
