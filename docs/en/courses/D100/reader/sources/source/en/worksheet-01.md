# Worksheet 1 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01}

## Practice exercises {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-practice}

### Exercise 1.1 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-01}

<!-- upstream_entity: Lineare Algebra/Nichtlineare Gleichungen/Aufgabe -->

Linear algebra often deals with systems of linear equations. What nonlinear equations or systems of equations arise in linear algebra?

### Exercise 1.2 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-02}

<!-- upstream_entity: Algebraische ebene Kurven/Beispiele/1/Skizziere/Aufgabe -->

Sketch the solution sets in $\mathbb R^2$ of the following equations.

1. $x^2-y^2-1=0$;
2. $x^2+xy+y^2=0$;
3. $x^2+y^2+1=0$;
4. $x^2+y^2=0$;
5. $x^2+y^3=0$;
6. $x^3-y^5=0$;
7. $x^2-x^3=0$;
8. $x^3+y^3=1$;
9. $x^4+y^4=1$;
10. $-5+3x+4x^2+x^3-y^2=1$.

### Exercise 1.3 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-03}

<!-- upstream_entity: Algebraische ebene Kurven/Beispiele/1/Schnitte mit Geraden/Aufgabe -->

Calculate the intersection of each curve in Exercise 1.2 with each of the following lines.

1. $x=0$;
2. $y=0$;
3. $x=1$;
4. $y=-2$;
5. $x=y$;
6. $x=-y$;
7. $2x-3y+4=0$.

### Exercise 1.4 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-04}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene affine Kurve/Y^2-X^3+2/Ganzzahliger Punkt und rationaler Punkt/Aufgabe -->

1. Find an integer solution $(x,y)\in\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$ of

   $$
   x^2-y^3+2=0.
   $$

2. Show that

   $$
   \left(\frac{383}{1000},\frac{129}{100}\right)
   $$

   is a solution of

   $$
   x^2-y^3+2=0.
   $$

### Exercise 1.5 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-05}

<!-- upstream_entity: Kubische Kurve/Finde einen Punkt/C/Aufgabe -->

Find a point on the plane algebraic curve

$$
V(X^3-Y^3+4X^2-2XY+Y+3)\subset\mathbb C^2.
$$

### Exercise 1.6 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-06}

<!-- upstream_entity: Neilsche Parabel/Bildbeschreibung durch Gleichung/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field. The image of the curve defined by

$$
K\longrightarrow K^2,
\qquad t\longmapsto(t^2,t^3),
$$

is called *Neil's parabola*. Show that a point $(x,y)\in K^2$ belongs to this image if and only if it satisfies $x^3=y^2$.

### Exercise 1.7 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-07}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomiale ebene Kurven/(t^2-t+2,t^2-3)/Kurvengleichung/Aufgabe -->

Let $C\subseteq\mathbb R^2$ be the image of the polynomial map

$$
\mathbb R\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2,
\qquad t\longmapsto(t^2-t+2,t^2-3).
$$

Find a nonzero polynomial $F$ in two variables such that $C$ lies in the zero locus of $F$.

### Exercise 1.8 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-08}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomiale ebene Kurven/(t^3-1,t^2-1)/Kurvengleichung/Aufgabe -->

Let $C\subseteq\mathbb R^2$ be the image of the polynomial map

$$
\mathbb R\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2,
\qquad t\longmapsto(t^3-1,t^2-1).
$$

Find a nonzero polynomial $F$ in two variables such that $C$ lies in the zero locus of $F$.

### Exercise 1.9 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-09}

<!-- upstream_entity: Differenzierbare Kurve/t^2-1,t^3-t/y^2 ist x^2+x^3/Aufgabe -->

Consider the curve

$$
\mathbb R\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2,
\qquad t\longmapsto(t^2-1,t^3-t).
$$

a. Show that the image points $(x,y)$ of this curve satisfy

   $$
   y^2=x^2+x^3.
   $$

b. Show that every point $(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2$ satisfying $y^2=x^2+x^3$ belongs to the image of the curve.

c. Show that there are exactly two parameter values $t_1$ and $t_2$ with the same image point, and that the map is otherwise injective.

### Exercise 1.10 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-10}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene algebraische Kurve/Satz über implizite Funktionen/Zusammenhang/Aufgabe -->

Discuss the relationship between plane algebraic curves and the implicit function theorem.

### Exercise 1.11 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-11}

<!-- upstream_entity: Algebraische Kurve/X^2Y + 2Y^3+3Y^2 ist 0/Punkte über Z mod 7/Aufgabe -->

Let $K=\mathbb Z/(7)$. Find all points in $K^2=K\times K$ lying on the curve defined by

$$
X^2Y+2Y^3+3Y^2=0.
$$

How many solutions are there?

### Exercise 1.12 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-12}

<!-- upstream_entity: Fermat-Kubik/Affin/Gerade/Ein Schnittpunkt/Aufgabe -->

Find a line $G\subseteq\mathbb A_{\mathbb C}^2$ meeting the curve

$$
C=V(X^3+Y^3+1)\subseteq\mathbb A_{\mathbb C}^2
$$

in exactly one point.

### Exercise 1.13 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-13}

<!-- upstream_entity: Neilsche Parabel/(1,1)/Gerade trifft/Aufgabe -->

Show that Neil's parabola

$$
C=V(Y^2-X^3)\subseteq\mathbb A_{\mathbb C}^2
$$

meets every line through $P=(1,1)\in C$ in at least one further point.

### Exercise 1.14 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-14}

<!-- upstream_entity: Neilsche Parabel/Einheitskreis/Schnittpunkt numerisch/Aufgabe -->

Give an analytic argument showing that the unit circle $V(x^2+y^2-1)$ and Neil's parabola $V(y^2-x^3)$ have a real intersection point. Determine numerically the real $x$-coordinate of such a point with error $\le 0.1$.

### Exercise 1.15 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-15}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene algebraische Kurven/Kubiken der Form x^2 ist F(y)/Skizziere für verschiedene Vorzeichen/Aufgabe -->

Consider equations of the form

$$
y^2=G(x),
\qquad G(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx+c,
$$

over $\mathbb R$. Sketch the different solution sets for coefficients $a,b,c\in\{1,-1,0\}$.

The statement in the next exercise also follows from Theorem 6.1 using Lemma 1.3.

### Exercise 1.16 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-16}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomiale Kurve/Bild/Schnitt mit Gerade/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field and let

$$
K\longrightarrow K^2,
\qquad t\longmapsto(P(t),Q(t))
$$

be a map given by two polynomials $P(t),Q(t)\in K[t]$. Let $B$ be its image and $G\subseteq K^2$ a line. Show that either $B\subseteq G$ or the intersection $B\cap G$ is finite.

### Exercise 1.17 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-17}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring in drei Variablen/Z/Multipliziere x^5+3x^2y^2-xyz^3 und 2x^3yz+z^2+5xy^2z-x^2y/Aufgabe -->

Multiply the following two polynomials in $\mathbb Z[x,y,z]$:

$$
x^5+3x^2y^2-xyz^3
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
2x^3yz+z^2+5xy^2z-x^2y.
$$

### Exercise 1.18 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-18}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomring in zwei Variablen/Z mod 5/Multipliziere x^4+2x^2y^2-xy^3+2y^3 und x^4y+4x^2y+3xy^2-x^2y^2+2y^2/Aufgabe -->

Multiply the following two polynomials in $(\mathbb Z/(5))[x,y]$:

$$
x^4+2x^2y^2-xy^3+2y^3
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
x^4y+4x^2y+3xy^2-x^2y^2+2y^2.
$$

### Exercise 1.19 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-19}

<!-- upstream_entity: Integritätsbereich/Polynomring/Integer/Aufgabe -->

Let $R$ be an integral domain. Show that the polynomial ring $R[X]$ is also an integral domain.

### Exercise 1.20 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-20}

<!-- upstream_entity: Integritätsbereich/Polynomring/Einheiten/Aufgabe -->

Let $R$ be an integral domain and $R[X]$ the polynomial ring over $R$. Show that the units of $R[X]$ are precisely the units of $R$.

### Exercise 1.21 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-21}

<!-- upstream_entity: Algebraisch abgeschlossener Körper/Charakterisierung mit Linearfaktoren/Fakt/Beweis/Aufgabe -->

Let $K$ be a field. Show that the following two properties are equivalent.

1. $K$ is algebraically closed.
2. Every nonconstant polynomial $F\in K[X]$ factors into linear factors.

### Exercise 1.22 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-22}

### Exercise 1.26 — 3 points {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-26}

<!-- upstream_entity: Ebene algebraische Kurven/Kreisgleichung/Punkte über endlichen Körpern/K ist F_2, F_5, F_11/Aufgabe -->

Find all solutions of the circle equation

$$
x^2+y^2=1
$$

for the fields $K=\mathbb Z/(2)$, $\mathbb Z/(5)$ and $\mathbb Z/(11)$.

### Exercise 1.27 — 5 points {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-27}

<!-- upstream_entity: Polynomiale ebene Kurven/(t^3-t^2+4t+3,-t^2+5t-1)/Kurvengleichung/Aufgabe -->

Let $C\subseteq\mathbb C^2$ be the image of the polynomial map

$$
\mathbb C\longrightarrow\mathbb C^2,
\qquad t\longmapsto(t^3-t^2+4t+3,-t^2+5t-1).
$$

Find a nonzero polynomial $F$ in two variables such that $C$ lies in the zero locus of $F$.

### Exercise 1.28 — 4 points {#br-ak-2025-2026-w01-ex-28}

<!-- upstream_entity: Einheitskreis/Rationale Parametrisierung/Funktionaler Ausdruck/Aufgabe -->

Consider the map

$$
f:\mathbb R\longrightarrow S^1\subseteq\mathbb R^2
$$

assigning to each $t\in\mathbb R$ the unique intersection point other than $(0,-1)$ of the line $G_t$ through $(t,1)$ and $(0,-1)$ with the unit circle

$$
S^1=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2\mid x^2+y^2=1\}.
$$

Show that this map is well defined and find the formulas describing it. Show that $f$ is differentiable. Is $f$ injective? Is $f$ surjective?
