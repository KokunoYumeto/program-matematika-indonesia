### Exercise 21.1 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w21-ex-01}

Let

$$
M\subseteq\mathbb N
$$

be a numerical monoid generated by relatively prime generators, and let $K[M]$ be its monoid ring over a field $K$. Write $M_+=M\setminus\{0\}$ and let

$$
R=K[M]_{\mathfrak m}
$$

be the localisation at the maximal ideal

$$
\mathfrak m=K[M_+]
=\left\langle T^m\mathrel{\Big|}m\in M_+\right\rangle.
$$

Show that $R$ is a discrete valuation ring only in the case $M=\mathbb N$.

*Source correction note:* In the last expression for $\mathfrak m$, the source indexes the generators by all $m\in M$, which would include $T^0=1$ and give the entire ring. The index is corrected to $m\in M_+$ to describe the intended ideal of positive monomials.

### Exercise 21.2 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w21-ex-02}

Let $R$ be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m=(p)$. Show that the order function

$$
\operatorname{ord}:R\setminus\{0\}\longrightarrow\mathbb N
$$

has the following properties.

1. For all $f,g\in R\setminus\{0\}$,

   $$
   \operatorname{ord}(fg)
   =\operatorname{ord}(f)+\operatorname{ord}(g).
   $$

2. For all $f,g\in R\setminus\{0\}$ with $f+g\ne0$,

   $$
   \operatorname{ord}(f+g)
   \geq\min\{\operatorname{ord}(f),\operatorname{ord}(g)\}.
   $$

3. For every $f\in R\setminus\{0\}$, we have $f\in\mathfrak m$ if and only if $\operatorname{ord}(f)\geq1$.
4. For every $f\in R\setminus\{0\}$, we have $f\in R^\times$ if and only if $\operatorname{ord}(f)=0$.

*Source correction note:* The condition $f+g\ne0$ is added in part 2 because the stated domain of $\operatorname{ord}$ does not contain $0$.

### Exercise 21.6 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w21-ex-06}

Let $K$ be a field with $\operatorname{char}(K)\ne2$, and let

$$
V=V(x^2+y^2-1)\subseteq\mathbb A_K^2
$$

be the unit circle over $K$. Let $P=(a,b)\in V$ be a point.

1. Show that the local ring $R$ of $V$ at $P$ is a discrete valuation ring.
2. Deduce that the coordinate ring

   $$
   K[X,Y]/(X^2+Y^2-1)
   $$

   is normal.
3. For this part, assume in addition that $-1$ is not a square in $K$. Show that

   $$
   K[X,Y]/(X^2+Y^2-1)
   $$

   is not a unique factorisation domain.
4. Determine the orders of $X$ and $Y-1$ in the local ring at $(0,1)$.

*Source correction note:* The source places no restriction on the characteristic and claims that the ring in part 3 is always nonfactorial. In characteristic $2$, the curve equation is a square and the coordinate ring is nonreduced. If $\operatorname{char}(K)\ne2$, the ring is factorial exactly when $-1$ is a square in $K$; the statement above adds the minimal hypotheses making all four parts true. The source's permission to assume $K$ algebraically closed is also removed because it conflicts with part 3.

### Exercise 21.8 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w21-ex-08}

Let $K$ be a field and

$$
\nu:(K^\times,\cdot,1)\longrightarrow(\mathbb Z,+,0)
$$

a surjective group homomorphism satisfying

$$
\nu(f+g)\geq\min\{\nu(f),\nu(g)\}
$$

for all $f,g\in K^\times$ with $f+g\ne0$. Show that

$$
R=\{f\in K^\times\mid\nu(f)\geq0\}\cup\{0\}
$$

is a discrete valuation ring.

*Source correction note:* The condition $f+g\ne0$ is added because $\nu$ is defined only on $K^\times$.

### Exercise 21.9 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w21-ex-09}

Let $f\in\mathbb C[X]$ with $f\ne0$ and $a\in\mathbb C$. Show that the following three “orders” of $f$ at $a$ coincide.

1. The order of vanishing of $f$ at $a$, namely the least order of a derivative satisfying

   $$
   f^{(k)}(a)\ne0.
   $$

2. The exponent of the linear factor $X-a$ in the factorisation of $f$ into irreducible polynomials.
3. The order of $f$ in the localisation $\mathbb C[X]_{(X-a)}$ of $\mathbb C[X]$ at the maximal ideal $(X-a)$.

The preceding exercise can be extended to other base fields using formal differentiation. Let $K$ be a field and $K[X]$ the polynomial ring over $K$. For a polynomial

$$
F=\sum_{i=0}^n a_iX^i\in K[X],
$$

the polynomial

$$
F'=na_nX^{n-1}+(n-1)a_{n-1}X^{n-2}+\cdots
   +3a_3X^2+2a_2X+a_1
$$

is called the **formal derivative** of $F$.
