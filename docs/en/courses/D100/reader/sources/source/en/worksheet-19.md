$$

### Exercise 19.7 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w19-ex-07}

Let

$$
P=X^2-3X+7
$$

and

$$
Q=Y^3-Y^2+4Y-5.

$$
\mathbb Z\subseteq\mathbb Z[X,Y]/(P,Q)

### Exercise 19.10 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w19-ex-10}

Let

$$
\varphi:R\longrightarrow S
$$

be an integral homomorphism of commutative rings, and let $R\longrightarrow R'$ be another ring homomorphism. Show that the base-changed homomorphism

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varphi':R'&\longrightarrow R'\otimes_R S,\\
f&\longmapsto f\otimes1
\end{aligned}
$$

is also integral.

### Exercise 19.11 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w19-ex-11}

For every field $K$ of characteristic $\ne2$, show that the ring homomorphism

$$
\begin{aligned}
K[X,Y]/(X^2+Y^2-1)&\longrightarrow
K[U,V]/(U^2+V^2-1),\\
(X,Y)&\longmapsto(U^2-V^2,2UV)
\end{aligned}
$$

is integral.

### Exercise 19.12 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w19-ex-12}

Let $K$ be a field and suppose a ring extension finite as a $K[X]$-module is given in the form

$$
K[X]\subseteq
K[X][Y]/\left(Y^n+P_{n-1}(X)Y^{n-1}+\cdots+P_2(X)Y^2+P_1(X)Y+P_0(X)\right)
=R,
$$

with $P_i(X)\in K[X]$, where the extension ring $R$ is an integral domain. Choose $k$ such that the degree of $P_i$ is at most $k(n-i)$ for all

$$
i=0,1,\ldots,n-1.
$$

Show that $YX^{-k}$ satisfies an equation of integral dependence of degree $n$ over $K[X^{-1}]$, and that the extension algebra

$$
K[X^{-1},YX^{-k}]
$$

has the same field of fractions as $R$.
