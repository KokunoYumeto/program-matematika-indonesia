## Source solution to Exercise 3.1 {#br-bgk-2019-w03-ex01-solution}

The Kronecker product of the two matrices is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{pmatrix}3&-4\\5&-2\end{pmatrix}
\otimes
\begin{pmatrix}-2&7\\6&3\end{pmatrix}
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
3\begin{pmatrix}-2&7\\6&3\end{pmatrix}&
-4\begin{pmatrix}-2&7\\6&3\end{pmatrix}\\[6pt]
5\begin{pmatrix}-2&7\\6&3\end{pmatrix}&
-2\begin{pmatrix}-2&7\\6&3\end{pmatrix}
\end{pmatrix}\\[8pt]
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
-6&21&8&-28\\
18&9&-24&-12\\
-10&35&4&-14\\
30&15&-12&-6
\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
