$$
C=V(Y^2-X^3)\subseteq\mathbb A_{\mathbb C}^2
$$

and the point $P=(1,1)\in C$. Show that the maximal ideal associated with $P$, namely the ideal $(X-1,Y-1)$ in the coordinate ring

$$
R=\mathbb C[X,Y]/(Y^2-X^3),
$$

cannot be described as the radical of an ideal generated by a single element $f\in R$.

The preceding exercise shows that every curve $D$ meeting Neil's parabola at $(1,1)$ also meets it at at least one other point. This also generalises Exercise 1.13.

### Exercise 18.11 ★ {#br-ak-2025-2026-w18-ex-11}

Consider the submonoid of the natural numbers generated by $2$ and $3$, namely

$$
M=\{0,2,3,4,5,\ldots\}\subset\mathbb N,
$$

and the quotient ring

$$
R=\mathbb Z/(9).
$$

Also consider the sets of $R$-valued points of $M$ and $\mathbb N$, namely the sets of monoid homomorphisms $\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(M,R)$ and $\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(\mathbb N,R)$, and the restriction map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\psi:\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(\mathbb N,R)
&\longrightarrow\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(M,R),\\
\pi&\longmapsto\pi|_M.
\end{aligned}
$$

1. Determine the units and nilpotent elements of $R$.
2. Determine the number of elements of $\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(\mathbb N,R)$.
3. Show that every $\rho\in\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(M,R)$ for which $\rho(2)$ is a unit in $R$ has an extension to $\mathbb N$.
4. Determine all $\pi\in\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(\mathbb N,R)$ whose restriction to $M\setminus\{0\}$ is the zero function.
5. Determine all $\rho\in\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(M,R)$ that have no extension to $\mathbb N$.
6. Determine the number of elements of $\operatorname{Mor}_{\mathrm{mon}}(M,R)$.

### Exercise 18.13 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w18-ex-13}

Let $M$ be a numerical monoid and $K$ a field. Show that there is an $f\in K[M]$ such that

$$
K[M]_f=K[X]_X.
$$

**Edition note:** this equality inside $K(X)$ requires that the generators
of $M$ have greatest common divisor $1$, a hypothesis omitted here in the
source. For $M=2\mathbb N$, every such localisation remains inside
$K(X^2)$ and cannot contain $X$.

<!-- upstream_entity: Numerisches Monoid/Monoidring/Polynomring/Aufgabe -->

### Exercise 18.14 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w18-ex-14}

Let $M$ be a numerical monoid, $K$ a field, and $K[M]$ the associated monoid ring. Show that

$$
K[M]\cong K[\mathbb N]
$$

if and only if $M=\mathbb N$.

**Edition note:** assume here that the generators of $M$ have greatest
common divisor $1$. With the broader definition of numerical monoid used
at the start of the lecture, the source's assertion is false:
$K[2\mathbb N]=K[X^2]\cong K[X]$, although $2\mathbb N\ne\mathbb N$.

### Exercise 18.16 {#br-ak-2025-2026-w18-ex-16}

Let $M$ be a commutative monoid and $K$ a field.

1. Show that the diagonal map

   $$
   \begin{aligned}
   \Delta:M&\longrightarrow M\times M,\\
   m&\longmapsto(m,m)
   \end{aligned}
   $$

   is a monoid homomorphism.
2. Describe the associated $K$-algebra homomorphism

   $$
   K[M]\longrightarrow K[M\times M]
   \cong K[M]\otimes K[M].
   $$

3. Describe the associated spectrum map

   $$
   K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(K[M])\times
   K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(K[M])
   \longrightarrow K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(K[M]).
   $$

   In particular, show that this makes $K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(K[M])$ itself a commutative monoid.

$$
P\in K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(M)

1. Show that there is a continuous map

   $$
   \begin{aligned}
   \Psi_P:K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(M)
   &\longrightarrow K\!-\!\operatorname{Spek}(M),\\
   Q&\longmapsto Q\cdot P.
   \end{aligned}

2. Describe the associated $K$-algebra homomorphism $K[M]\longrightarrow K[M]$.

### Exercise 18.25 (3 points) {#br-ak-2025-2026-w18-ex-25}

Let $M\subseteq\mathbb N$ be a numerical monoid and $K$ a field. Define

$$
M_+=M\cap\mathbb N_+

$$
nM_+=\left\{m\in M\mathrel{\Big|}
