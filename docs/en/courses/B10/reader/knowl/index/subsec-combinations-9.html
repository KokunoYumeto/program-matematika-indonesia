Theorem 3.4.9. Closed Formula for \(\binom{n}{k}\).

\begin{equation*} \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!k!} = \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)\cdots(n-(k-1))}{k(k-1)(k-2)\cdots 1}\text{.} \end{equation*}
