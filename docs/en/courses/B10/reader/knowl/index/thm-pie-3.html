Theorem 3.3.6. Cardinality of a Union (3 Sets).

For any finite sets \(A\text{,}\) \(B\text{,}\) and \(C\text{,}\)
\begin{align*} \card{A \cup B \cup C} = \amp \card{A} + \card{B} + \card{C} \\ - \amp \card{A \cap B} - \card{A \cap C} - \card{B \cap C} \\ + \amp \card{A \cap B \cap C} \text{.} \end{align*}
