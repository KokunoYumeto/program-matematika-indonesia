Exercise 16.

Let \(t_n\) denote the number of ways to tile a \(2\times n\) chessboard using \(1\times 2\) dominoes. Write out the first few terms of the sequence \((t_n)_{n \ge 1}\text{,}\) and then give a recursive definition. Explain why your recursive formula is correct.
🔗
Hint.
There is only one way to tile a \(2 \times 1\) board, and two ways to tile a \(2\times 2\) board (you can orient the dominoes in two ways). In general, consider the two ways the domino covering the top left corner could be oriented.
