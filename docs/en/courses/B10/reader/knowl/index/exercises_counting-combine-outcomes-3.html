Exercise 2.

The number 735000 factors as \(2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^4 \cdot 7^2\text{.}\) How many divisors does it have? Explain your answer using the multiplicative principle.
🔗
Hint.
For a simpler example, there are 4 divisors of \(6 = 2\cdot 3\text{.}\) They are \(1 = 2^0\cdot 3^0\text{,}\) \(2 = 2^1\cdot 3^0\text{,}\) \(3 = 2^0\cdot 3^1\text{,}\) and \(6 = 2^1\cdot 3^1\text{.}\)
