# The Science of Computing

Victor Eijkhout, with Edmond Chow and Robert van de Geijn: *The Science of Computing*, volume 1 of *The Art of High
Performance Computing*, 3rd edition (2022), formatted 15 January 2026, 487 pages: computer architecture and
parallel computing, floating-point arithmetic, numerical treatment of differential equations, numerical linear
algebra (direct and iterative methods, conjugate gradients, GMRES, preconditioning), applications and appendices.
