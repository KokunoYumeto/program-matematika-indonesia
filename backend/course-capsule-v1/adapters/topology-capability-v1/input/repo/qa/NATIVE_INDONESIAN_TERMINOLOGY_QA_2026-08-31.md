# Native Indonesian topology terminology QA — 2026-08-31

This one-time post-completion check compared the edition's terminology with bounded Indonesian university-publisher evidence. No comparison work was transcribed or imported.

## Evidence

- Solikhin, *Buku Ajar Topologi*, UNDIP Press, catalog record 590: the official `Synopsis` uses *topologi*, *fungsi kontinu*, *ekuivalen (secara topologi)*, *dwikontinu*, *geometri karet*, and *teori himpunan*. <https://penerbit.undip.ac.id/index.php/penerbit/catalog/book/590>
- *Ruang Topologi Fuzzy*, UNDIP Press, catalog record 948: the official `Synopsis` uses *ruang topologi*, *topologi relatif*, *fungsi kontinu*, *image*, *preimage*, and the variant *homeomorphisma*. <https://penerbit.undip.ac.id/index.php/penerbit/catalog/book/948>
- Kusrini, *Pengantar Topologi*, Module 1, Universitas Terbuka, 2011, pp. 1–51, ISBN 9789790115002: the official host's indexed opening snippet attests *korespondensi satu-satu*, *himpunan terurut parsial*, and *elemen*. <https://repository.ut.ac.id/4753/> The official PDF body could not be fetched because the host rejected/reset direct access, so these are explicitly snippet-level attestations and no body-page citation is claimed.

## Result

- Four occurrences of *ruang topologis* were corrected to the directly attested *ruang topologi*; zero remain across the reader sources and companions.
- The edition retains *citra*, *prapeta*, and *homeomorfisme* as consistent Indonesian house terms while recording the witnessed alternatives *image*, *preimage*, and *homeomorphisma*.
- *Topologi relatif* remains a source-sensitive synonym for *topologi subruang*.
- The glossary now records *dwikontinu*, *korespondensi satu-satu*, *urutan parsial*, and *himpunan terurut parsial*; *unsur* remains the formal default, with *elemen* allowed in natural collocations and *anggota* reserved for membership.
- Whole-reader schema, completion-module schema, and full-corpus closure QA all pass after propagation.
